How your file was used

  • Only the order of your wins and losses is taken from the file. The amounts are re-sized to the TP, SL and ladder in the rail, so all three lines are compared like for like.
  • Your own position sizing, your own TP and SL, and any manual intervention are deliberately discarded. This is not a replay of your account.
  • The bot would never have taken your trades. It has its own entry logic, so a real run would produce a different sequence with a similar shape.
  • Deposits, withdrawals and other balance operations are skipped. Commission and swap are folded into each trade where the file reports them.

What you are watching

  • Your settings and Your uploaded history are simulations. Sizes come from the recovery array in the rail, and each result comes from the TP and SL beside it, so editing the array changes the replay immediately.
  • Your uploaded history keeps the real order of your wins and losses and re-sizes it. It does not replay your actual position sizes.
  • Real SOS fills is the only source showing genuine broker records: real entries, exits, stops, targets and lot sizes from 4 September 2026. Nothing there is modelled.
  • For simulated sources the price path is drawn from your start price and stop distance, stepping one stop per loss. It is the geometry your settings imply, not real price action — only Real SOS fills shows prices a broker actually printed.
